Common questions

What does SMTFinder detect?

SMTFinder detects SMT divergence by comparing your active chart symbol against one or two correlated markets. It highlights when one market takes a key high or low and the other fails to confirm.

Who is this for?

It is built for traders who use SMT, liquidity concepts, market structure, or ICT-style confirmation and want cleaner execution context without manual chart comparison.

Does it support higher timeframe SMTs too?

Yes. In addition to intraday session and cycle SMTs, SMTFinder includes HTF SMT detection for daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, and yearly structure.
